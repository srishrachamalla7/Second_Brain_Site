Rather than theorise, Srish opened Google Analytics on Fauxtalks and walked through nine months of real data.

Where the traffic came from. Almost entirely Direct — people clicking the link in the Reddit post:

4. The reality check — branded search is not SEO

This is the sharpest part of the call, and it is a finding about Srish's own product, not a lesson delivered at Nishita.

Looking at Search Console queries for FauxTalks:

"నాకు వచ్చిన ట్రాఫిక్ అంతా ఫాక్స్ టాక్స్ పేరు అంటే పేరు తెలిసినోడే వస్తున్నాడు" — all the traffic I get is people searching the name FauxTalks. Only people who already know it are coming.

In other words: people who saw the Reddit post, or heard about it word of mouth, are typing the brand name. That is not search working — that is Reddit working, with a delay.

The keyword that matters is fake screenshot generator. Where FauxTalks stands on it:

   FauxTalks        not in the top 5. not ranking at all.
                    ~9.6K monthly visits

   Competitor       ~190K monthly visits
                    same idea, better execution:
                    messages, voice notes, video export, paid unlocks

The conclusion drawn: SEO comes from blogs. The competitor and sites like ShipRocket rank because they publish related content continuously — "ఈచ్ బ్లాక్ కి ద రిలేటెడ్ ఇన్ఫర్మేషన్ అలా పెడుతూ ఉంటా". Writing blogs, checking which queries come in, and monitoring what is being missed is the actual work.
