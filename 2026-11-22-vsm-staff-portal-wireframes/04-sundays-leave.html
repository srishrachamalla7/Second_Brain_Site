04 · Sundays & leave

Sundays & leave

About 83 staff are split into Batches A, B and C. The store opens at most 3 Sundays a month and each batch works one of them as a normal day, with no extra pay. Admin users record swaps and holiday Sundays here, and keep track of the 1 free leave a month: used, banked (up to 3) or cashed out on the next pay day. Both areas open from More. Tap the calendar days, rows, chips and buttons: links jump to the next screen on this page.

04-02

Sunday detail (roster)

Who was supposed to work this Sunday, who punched, who swapped and who is missing. The roster is the batch list with swaps applied: people swapped in are added, people swapped out show as Off.

  • A missing person (Absent) opens the change-with-reason sheet, e.g. they were sent outside and couldn't punch.
  • A person on Leave opens their leave history (04-10).
  • Chips filter the roster. Swap icon records a swap for this Sunday (04-05).
  • The audit card lists every change that touched this Sunday.

04-04

Mark a Sunday as a holiday

Opened from a Sunday or the ⋮ menu (shown open over December). Before saving, it previews how the rotation queue shifts, so nobody is surprised.

  • What changes: the due batch takes the next open Sunday, and every later batch moves one step. 27 Dec is the closed 4th Sunday, so Batch B goes to 3 Jan.
  • Switch: open 27 Dec instead, so B still works in December. The preview updates.
  • Swaps on that day move with the batch; the warning names who to tell.
  • Mark holiday saves, logs an audit row (reason, who, when) and hatches 6 Dec on the calendar.
Pending owner: Q-A (rotation queue vs. losing the turn). Also: when a holiday pushes a batch into the next month, should the app offer to open a closed Sunday? Do swaps move with the batch, or get cancelled?

04-05 · step 3

Record a swap: one-way or two-way

The two employees decide; the app supports both. Each option spells out who works on which date, so the admin can read it back to them.

  • Two-way: they trade Sundays. One-way: the partner covers; nobody owes a Sunday back.
  • Record swap is blocked until "Both agreed" is ticked and a reason is typed. On save: toast, both Sunday rosters update, and an audit row appears in the swap log (04-06).
One-way swaps: the partner works two Sundays and the other person none. Still no extra pay for the extra Sunday, and nothing deducted from the person who skipped? Owner to confirm.

Leave overview (month)

Opens from More → Leave. For each employee: was this month's free day used, their banked balance (x/3), and whether a bank-or-cash-out choice is still pending. It opens on "To decide" because that is the work before month end.

  • Decide opens the end-of-month choice (04-08; 04-08b when the bank is full).
  • A name opens that person's leave history (04-10).
  • Calendar-plus applies leave for someone (04-09). Chips filter; Deductions lists absences beyond free and banked leave.

End-of-month leave decision

The employee chooses what happens to an unused free day. The admin records it; the sheet shows both outcomes in plain numbers.

  • Bank it: shows the balance before and after (max 3).
  • Cash out: the amount and the pay day it lands on (the next pay day after month end), with how the daily rate is worked out.
  • Save choice needs a note, writes an audit row and updates the list.
