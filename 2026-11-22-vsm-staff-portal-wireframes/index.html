VSM Staff Portal · Wireframes

Wireframes for the owner's review

Mid-fidelity, phone first (390 × 844). Real layout, colours and type, but every name and amount is invented. Each page holds one group of screens. Under each screen, a caption says what it is for and what happens on tap. Draft of 23 Nov 2026.

01

Access & dashboard

Install to home screen, Face ID / fingerprint login, password fallback, the Home dashboard and the More menu.

 02

Employees & salary

Staff list, employee profile, add/edit/deactivate, bulk import, device-code mapping, salary changes and increment history.

 03

Attendance

Upload the biometric export, day and month views, fix incomplete punches, short-day flags, overrides with reason, unmatched codes.

 04

Sundays & leave

The 3 batches, Sunday rotation calendar, holiday Sundays, one-way and two-way swaps, free leave: bank it or cash it out.

 05

Payroll, payslips & ledger

Biweekly pay runs (2nd and 4th Saturday), payouts, the payslip (A4 and 80 mm, share to WhatsApp, print), the employee ledger.

 K

Kit example

Reference screens for designers: dashboard, the override-with-reason sheet, loading/error/empty states, desktop layout.

Navigation map

Four tabs plus More at the bottom of the phone. On a desktop (1024 px and wider) the same items sit in a left rail.
