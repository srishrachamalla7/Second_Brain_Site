05 · Payroll, payslips & ledger

Payroll, payslips & ledger

The Pay tab. Flow: open the next pay day, review the run (Incomplete days block it), check one person's breakdown, record payouts, then print or share payslips. Every generate, print, share and manual change is logged. The ledger keeps a running balance per employee. All names and amounts are invented.

Assumptions used here: the 4th Saturday pays 1–15 of the same month and the 2nd Saturday pays 16–end of the previous month, so there is time to upload attendance and fix punches. Each run pays half the monthly salary, less absences at the daily rate. Working days in November = 25 Mon–Sat + 1 batch Sunday = 26, so ₹15,600 a month gives ₹600 a day.

05-04

Move pay date (reason sheet)

Any admin user can move one pay day. Same override-with-reason pattern as every other manual change.

  • Quick chips: day before and next working day; Other date… opens the calendar picker.
  • Move is blocked until a reason is typed. On save: toast, the Pay home card shows the new date with "moved from Sat 28 Nov", and an audit row is written.
  • The pay period (1–15 Nov) does not change, only the date paid.

Record payouts (bulk or one by one)

Shown on pay day evening, after the run is reviewed. Tick who was paid, then say how, when and by whom.

  • Segmented: Unpaid / Part-paid / Paid. The bar shows progress for the run.

  • Each payout writes a ledger row and an audit row. The run becomes Paid when nothing is left unpaid.

Payslip preview, A4

Plain invoice look in black and white. No store name, no logo, no address. Only the slip number, the employee, the period, the date paid and the amount.

  • Working-hours table: days, Short days, absent, leave (free / banked), hours. No allowances.
  • Amount table: half salary, absence cut, cash-out added, advance recovered, net paid in figures and words. Two signature lines.
  • 80 mm thermal switches to 05-07. Print opens a sheet (paper, copies). WhatsApp opens the phone's share sheet. Both are logged.

05-09

Employee ledger

Running statement, newest first. Salary owed and advance outstanding are kept as two balances so an advance never hides unpaid salary.

  • Earned and additions raise the salary balance; deductions, advance recovered and payouts lower it.
  • Advance given only changes the advance balance ("Adv").
  • Grey row = scheduled for the next pay day, not yet posted.
  • Chips filter by type; date field sets the period; download icon exports PDF or Excel. Try it.
Reached from More → Ledger (pick an employee) and from the Ledger tab of the staff profile.

05-10

Pay reports

Four short reports in tabs. Each exports to PDF or Excel from the download icon.

  • Payroll: month summary, both runs, payout modes, totals by department.
  • Sundays: extra Sunday pay before batches against the plan with batches. This is the saving the batches are meant to bring.
  • Advances: who owes what and how it will be recovered.
  • Cash-out: banked leave held (cap 3 days) and what it would cost if everyone cashed out.
The "before" Sunday figures need the owner's past Sunday pay records (Aug–Nov). Where do they come from?
