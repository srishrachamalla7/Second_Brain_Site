Employees & salary

The Staff tab: find a person, open their profile, edit or deactivate them, bring the first employee list in from Excel, change a salary (owner only) and give or track salary advances. Every manual change asks for a reason and lands in the employee's History tab. All names, phone numbers and amounts are invented.

02-01

Staff list (Staff tab)

Find anyone in two taps. Each row: name, VSM number, department, batch and the latest day's status (icon + word + colour).

  • Search matches name, VSM number (31, 031 or VSM/031) and phone.

02-02

Employee profile (six tabs, all clickable)

Everything about one person. The tabs scroll sideways on a phone.

  • Overview: phone (tap to call), department, joined date, batch and next Sunday, shift, and linked device codes. "Unlink" asks nothing more because the old code has no punches; unlinking a code with punches uses the reason sheet.
  • Attendance: this month's calendar with counts. Dot on 18 Nov = edited. "Open full month" goes to 03.
  • Pay: current salary, working days, daily rate (salary ÷ working days), last two increments. "Change salary" opens 02-09; "See all" opens 02-10.
  • Leave: this month's free leave, banked days (max 3), month by month. Bank / cash out lives in 04.
  • Advances: balance left, each advance (opens 02-14), "Give advance" (02-13).
  • History: this person's audit trail, filterable.
  • Pencil opens 02-03. ⋮ opens an actions sheet with Deactivate at the bottom (02-05).

02-03

Add / edit employee (one form)

The same form adds a new person (empty, next VSM number filled in, salary field editable once) or edits one. Shown here in edit mode with two fields changed.

  • Changed fields show the old value under them.
  • Salary is read-only on edit; "Change" opens the owner's salary sheet (02-09).
  • Review 2 changes opens the reason sheet (02-04). Adding a new person saves straight away, with "Added by Padma" in History.
  • Device codes are linked on the profile (02-02) or from Attendance → unmatched codes.

02-04

Save changes with a reason (D-02 pattern)

Edits to an existing person go through the shared override sheet: each changed field old → new, then one required reason.

  • Save changes is blocked until a reason is typed (error under the field, as in D-02).
  • On save: toast, back to the profile, one audit row per field in History, all with the same reason.
  • Back returns to the form with the edits kept.

02-05

Deactivate with a reason

From the profile's ⋮ menu. Never a delete: the person moves to Inactive and drops out of attendance, batches and future pay runs.

  • Banner shows unpaid days and any advance balance, so nobody leaves owing or owed without it being seen.
  • Last working day opens the date picker; days after it are not counted.
  • Deactivate (danger) is blocked until details are typed. Toast offers Undo.

02-09

Change salary / increment (owner only)

The override-with-reason sheet, specialised for money: old → new, effective date, required reason, and a preview of the new daily rate.

  • New salary: whole rupees; the difference and % update as you type.
  • Effective from: 1st of next month by default; "16 Nov" (start of the current half) and any date are allowed.
  • Daily rate preview = salary ÷ working days that month (Mon–Sat plus the batch's one Sunday).
  • Save is blocked without a reason. The change appears in History (02-10) and on Home's "Recent changes".
  • Padma and Ramesh see the Pay tab but no sheet: the button is disabled with "Ask the Owner".
A change dated in the past (e.g. 16 Nov) would alter a pay run already paid. Proposed: allow it only for unpaid periods, and add any arrears to the next payout.

02-13

Give advance

Records money handed over today and how it comes back. Opens from the advance list, the Pay tab or a profile (employee prefilled there).

  • Employee opens a searchable picker.
  • Recovery plan: full from the next payout (default) or split over N payouts; the dates and amounts update as N changes. Uneven splits put the remainder in the last payout.
  • Given by defaults to the signed-in user. Reason is required.
  • The banner warns if recovery would take more than the payout; it never blocks.
  • Saved advances show on the payslip and in the ledger (05).
Should there be a limit, e.g. an advance no larger than one month's salary, or only one open advance per person?

Advance detail with recovery entries

One advance: what was given, by whom and why, what has come back and what is still scheduled.

  • Recovery: one line per payout, deducted or scheduled. Each deducted line links to its payslip.
