D-01

Dashboard (Home tab)

The first screen after Face ID / fingerprint login. It answers "what do I need to do today?" before showing totals.

  • Banner: the next pay date and what blocks payroll. Tap goes to the blocking list.
  • Needs attention rows: tap opens Attendance filtered to that status.
  • Attendance card: the last uploaded day. Tap opens that day in Attendance.
  • Next Sunday opens the Sunday calendar. Payout opens the draft payroll run.
  • Recent changes: audit rows. "See all" opens the Audit log.
  • More (5th tab) opens a sheet. Try it.

D-02

Override with reason (shared sheet)

The one pattern for every manual change: day status, missed punch, salary, leave, swap. Old value on top, new value, required reason.

  • Save is blocked until a reason is typed (error shown under the field, not as a popup).
  • On save: toast "Saved", the calendar day gets a small dot (.cal .d.edited) and an audit row is written.
  • Cancel, the X, swipe down or tapping the dim area closes it without saving.

D-03

Loading, error and empty states

Reference for the three states every list screen needs. Real screens show one at a time.

  • Loading: skeleton rows in the shape of the real rows, no spinner on a blank page.
  • Error: say what failed and give a Retry button in place.
  • Empty: say why it is empty and give the one action that fills it.
