1. What Altcrew is

Abhishikth's one-liner:

"On a single line, I can say we are actually replicating what Smedpals does in the US, we are getting Smedpals to India."

An event discovery and ticketing app, narrowed deliberately to health, fitness and wellness. Build communities, host events themselves, and collaborate with existing fitness communities and wellness groups as co-hosts.

His argument for why a crowded market does not matter:

"How many ever ticketing platforms that are coming up, I'm not worried about it because we have these three unique features… all the other apps take all the events, but we are just concentrating on fitness, health and wellness."

2. The three features

Flash Album — a 24-hour shared photo album per event

buy ticket --> arrive --> organiser SCANS ticket --> album UNLOCKS for that person
                                                        |
                           anyone at the event uploads -|
                           anyone at the event views ---|
                           download, repost to Instagram|
                                                        |
                           24 hours later --> album DELETED
                           (reminder notification before it goes)
"You bought a ticket outside and you come to the BookMyShow or Cinema… స్కాన్ చేస్తారు కదా, so this is the exact same thing when you come to the event, you scan the ticket and as soon as the ticket is scanned, that flash album for that person should be enabled."

Locked behind a real check-in scan — not a tap, not a geofence. Separate from the Google Wallet / Apple Wallet work, which Abhishikth said is for ticketing only.

Pace Files — removing the "will I look stupid" problem

The ASR writes it "paste files". From the content it is about pace; the spelling needs confirming.

The problem, in his words:

"Run clubs right now are nothing but basically dating clubs, right? So I have this inferiority complex… new people, new group, నన్ను ఎవరైనా జడ్జ్ చేస్తారా? These questions come up, people don't attend such events."
AT CHECKOUT   ask: what pace are you?  (athlete / casual runner / beginner)
              wordplay, not labels -- "are you a tortoise or a rabbit"

AFTER PAYING  dialog: "don't worry -- Sai, Rajesh, Mani and 16 others
              at the event are at your pace. Just find them there."

AT THE EVENT  ??? a ping / hi system so your pace group finds each other
              "Rajesh hi" -> Rajesh can send a hi back

That third box is ours to design:

"If it is a messaging, if it is pinging, I do not know how to execute that, so I want you guys to think of how we can execute that feature."

Shoe Locker — a mileage log for your sneakers

upload a photo of each pair --> pick the pair for today's event
                                     |
          ran 5K today --> 15 + 5 = 20 km total on that shoe
                                     |
          approaching ~500 km --> "use your other pair, this one is expiring"
          no pairs uploaded at all --> brand recommendation (Nike / Puma, if tied up)

He called the maths what it is — "it is very simple, it's a simple math". The 500 figure is his guess, stated as a guess.

And the rule that ties all three together

This is the line to keep:

"You have to think at such level that people will get to use all this features. ఎక్కడో సెట్టింగ్స్ లో పడేయకుండా." — don't bury them somewhere in settings.

His own example: ticket icon top-left → your events → tap one → QR code, and under the QR code the shoe picker pops up if the event involves shoes.

"I know people who are hosts here, so it is fine… but when it comes to such a big place, Hyderabad, some other places, we might not know everybody."

Srish pushed back on holding bank data, and this is a decision:

"పేమెంట్ గేట్వే ని హ్యాండిల్ చేస్తుంది. లెట్స్ సే స్ట్రైప్ ఆర్ రైజర్ పే… we can't take it అన్నమాట. అగైన్, మళ్ళీ మనకి ఒకవేళ అకౌంట్ నెంబర్ అవన్నీ తీసుకుంటే we have to follow RBI rules."

The payment gateway (Stripe or Razorpay) holds the account details and does the payouts. We do not store account numbers, because that drags in RBI data-residency rules. Abhishikth's answer was "ఓకే, చూద్దాం" — not a refusal, not an agreement either.

Refunds — parked

BookMyShow holds refunds around 15 days. Abhishikth thinks that is far too long and wants to promise 2 to 3 days, while noting the big platforms all take 7–10 working days. Explicitly deferred: "later on we can see what kind of a security system we can come up with."
