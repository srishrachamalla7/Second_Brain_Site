Getting sales done without hiring salespeople

An explainer of four websites (Salesforge, AiSDR, CommissionCrowd, RepHunter): what they do, what they have in common, and what we could build.

In one line: companies want more sales but can't afford full-time salespeople. Two sites answer with AI that does the selling, the other two with human reps paid only on commission. The opportunity is to combine both.

What all four share

💸 Pay for results, not salaries. Commission or pay-per-lead instead of a fixed salary.
🔗 Matching. A product meets someone, or something, that can sell it.
📊 Pipeline tracking. CRM, deal manager, HubSpot/Salesforce sync.
🏢 Companies pay. The rep or user side is free or cheap.

A company lists its product. AI finds the right commission-only reps. Every rep gets an AI assistant that fills their pipeline. Deals and commissions are tracked and paid inside the platform.

5 · Build plan

Three phases, about 6–8 months for the first two

Phase 1 · MVP marketplace (3–4 months)
  • Company onboarding: product, ideal customer, commission terms, territory
  • Rep profiles: industries, regions, accounts they already sell to
  • AI matching: score reps against opportunities, send weekly match emails
  • Chat, contract template, deal registration (protects the rep's commission)
  • Deal pipeline and commission payouts (Stripe Connect / Razorpay Route)
Phase 2 · AI toolkit for reps (3 months)
  • Lead finder through a data API (Apollo, People Data Labs). Don't build our own database.
  • AI research and personal email drafts, using the company's product material
  • Send through the rep's own Gmail/Outlook, with daily limits
  • One inbox for all replies, with AI tagging (interested / not now / no)
Phase 3 · Automation and extras
  • Fully automatic "AI rep" option for companies that want no humans
  • Rep reputation scores and reviews
  • Early commission advances for reps (CommissionCrowd lists this as "coming soon")
  • Paid recruiting service (RepHunter charges $6k–12k for this)
⚠️ Don't build: our own email-sending infrastructure (mailboxes, IPs, warm-up). Salesforge runs six products just for that. Plug into Instantly or Smartlead instead.

7 · Risks

What can go wrong, and the fix

🐔 Chicken and egg
No reps means no companies, and no companies means no reps.
Fix: launch in one industry and one region. Recruit 200+ reps before inviting companies.
🚪 Deals move off the platform
After meeting, the company and rep may cut us out.
Fix: deal registration, payouts only through us, contract protection for the rep.
📧 Spam and the law
Emails land in spam. CAN-SPAM, GDPR and India's DPDP Act apply. LinkedIn bans automation.
Fix: sending limits, opt-out links, use proven sending tools, no LinkedIn bots.
📉 Weak reps
Most commission-only reps quit within weeks.
Fix: vetting, reviews, activity scores, and the AI toolkit so they see results early.
⚖️ Contractor vs employee
If reps look like employees, there are tax and labour problems.
Fix: clear independent-contractor agreements, reviewed by a lawyer per country.
8 · Before building

Questions to ask the client

Tick them off as you get answers. Your ticks stay on this phone.

Words you'll hear

SDR: Sales Development Rep. The person who finds leads and books meetings.
ICP: Ideal Customer Profile. Who you want to sell to.
Deliverability: whether your emails reach the inbox or the spam folder.
Warm-up: slowly raising how many emails a new mailbox sends, so it isn't flagged as spam.
Take rate: the % a marketplace keeps from each deal.
Churn: % of customers who cancel each month.
ACV: Annual Contract Value. What one customer pays per year.
1099 rep: a US independent contractor, not an employee.
