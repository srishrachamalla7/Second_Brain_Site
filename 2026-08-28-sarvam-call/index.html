  • Call transfer (the missing feature): Sarvam partners with Gupshup

("Gobis") as its CPaaS. Transfer to a human/landline/SIP works on Gupshup infrastructure — testable by Monday if numbers are rented from Sarvam. Exotel ("Cleo"?) etc. on the roadmap, ~next month.

  • Pricing (current pay-as-you-go): ₹4.5/min per agent · ₹115/number/month

rental · +₹0.4/min for the human-transferred portion (30s pulse = ₹0.2) · ₹0.35/min for SIP-destination transfer. Worked example from the call: 3-min call (2 min agent + 1 min human) ≈ ₹1.6 telephony + ₹9 agent ≈ ₹10.2 total.

Including telephony" — the benchmark Sarvam pricing is being compared against.

New subscription pricing plans coming in ~2 weeks (lower per-minute rate).

org ID/email + the competitor proposal screenshot.

for transfers).
