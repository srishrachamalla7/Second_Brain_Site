Premium comes from hierarchy, useful content and predictable behavior—not extra decoration.

01

Web is the control room.

Create projects, manage credentials, inspect event attempts and understand usage. Keep setup and operations together.

02

Mobile is the action layer.

Quickly scan alerts, open the right context, and acknowledge a shared event. Avoid bringing every admin task onto the phone.

03

One language. Native behavior.

Share color and event semantics. Let iOS and Android keep their own navigation, typography, permissions and system controls.
