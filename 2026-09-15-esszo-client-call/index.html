1. Order modify — a bug nobody can find

This is the most serious thing on the call, and the most honest moment in it.

The order-edit feature is built. But after an order is modified, the system picks the wrong variant:

   BEFORE FIX          AFTER FIX
   ──────────          ─────────
   picks a RANDOM      picks the HIGHEST
   variant             variant

        │                   │
        └──── still wrong ──┘

Said plainly on the call:

"అది ఏ లైన్ ఆఫ్ కోడ్ వల్ల అవుతుందో మాకు అర్థం కావట్లేదు, ఎంత డీబగ్ చేసినా దొరకలేదు" — "we cannot understand which line of code is causing it; however much we debugged, we did not find it."

What was done instead of a fix. Rather than leave it live:

  • They checked the whole database. **Exactly one consumer had ever modified an

order.** That record's values were corrected by hand.

  • The API was removed, so nobody else can reach the path — *"ఎవ్వరూ ఇట్లా ఫ్రాడ్

చేయకూడదు, అంటే కావాల్సింది కాకపోయినా, మిస్టేక్ అయినా"* ("so nobody can commit fraud through this, whether deliberately or by mistake").

MY TAKE: disabling the endpoint was the right call, but it means order modify is currently unavailable, not working. The client is counting it as coming back on Sunday.

2. Negative profit in the distributor app

3. Push notifications — from order status to offers

Scenario-based notifications (abandoned cart and similar) are built. The client does not want those first.

What they want instead: notifications fired from the admin panel, for offers.

The reasoning is not technical, it is commercial:

There are about 50 customers, of whom roughly 10 are active. A notification is a reminder that the app exists on the phone at all. "లోపల సేల్స్ ఇంప్రూవ్ అయింది దాంతోటి" — "sales improved because of it."

How it must behave — same as banners:

   notification tapped
          │
          ├── product advertisement  →  product page / product card
          └── category advertisement →  category page

Scenario-based notifications are explicitly demoted to second priority.

ETA given: admin push notifications done by end of today or tomorrow.

WhatState

There was a genuine misunderstanding surfaced here: we had understood "enlarge" to mean on the product page; the client meant inside the order section. Agreed it can be done in the order section, and since the new distributor build has not gone out yet, it goes in now.

5. Distributor name next to the logo

6. Stock, and why accounting cannot be touched yet

The single biggest item, and the one everything else is queued behind.

Where they are today. Still working on paper:

"still date మేము print out తీసుకొని అందులో manual గా cut చేసి, ఇది ఈ stock లేదు అని cut చేసుకొని, minus total నుంచి minus చేసి, అది పరిస్థితి" — "to this day we take a printout, cut items out by hand, mark this stock is not there, subtract it from the total. That is the situation."

What is blocking it. The system was built as designed, but this scenario was never predicted. The problem is that real stock is not being maintained, so accounts cannot be balanced.

The agreed sequence — and this is the important decision of the call:

  1. ship the in-stock / out-of-stock fix        (Sunday)
  2. turn OFF "feature stock"                    ← the padded stock numbers
  3. load REAL inventory only
  4. NOW accounting starts tracking correctly
  5. whatever breaks after that, identify then

Until step 3, "accounting ని touch చేసినా గాని, it doesn't make sense" — touching accounting makes no sense.

Why "feature stock" exists at all: quantities were being uploaded higher than reality so that orders would not be missed. That is exactly what breaks the accounts.

Order modify rides on this. The partial-fulfilment case the client keeps asking for — customer orders 10, we have 5, invoice goes out for 5 — is part of the same Sunday delivery. The flows that need it:

  • admin → distributor admin modify
  • distributor → consumer distributor modify

Explicitly not the consumer side. The consumer does not modify.

7. Salesman tracking

Raised late in the call and prefaced with an apology — "very sorry for bringing this out very late." It is the only genuinely new requirement.

The problem. There is one marketing-cum-sales manager today. Another joins next week. There is no way to know who onboarded which shop.

The solution agreed, and it is small:

A salesman name field on consumer onboarding, reusing the slot where the referral code already sits. "ఒక column ఇస్తే అయిపోతుంది అంతే" — one column is all it takes.

What that unlocks, in the client's words: how many shops under each salesman, how much sale came from those shops, how much per month, and how much share that salesman is owed.

The reassignment question. Asked and answered well:

  • Salesmen leave. Their shops keep ordering. A replacement gets sent.
  • The analogy used: a medical rep leaves, the company appoints another and keeps

sending them to the same doctor.

  • Answer: the admin UI gets a way to reassign a shop from one salesman to

another, done manually. A fixed code (e.g. ABC) carrying forward to whoever holds the role was floated and set aside as more trouble than it is worth.

Priority: explicitly not urgent. "ఇప్పుడు అంత urgent ఏం లేదు అది" — plan it after the current enhancements. The near-term purpose is not share calculation, it is "వాడిని preserve పెట్టడానికి లేకపోతే వాడిని motivate చేయడానికి" — to retain and motivate the salesmen.

This came from our side, not theirs. We built a rental management application with agreement templates, and offered the same here.

Why now. The client has just given the entire old city area to a single distributor. An agreement would give distributors like her confidence in the arrangement. Admitted they had discussed it two or three times internally and never thought it through.
