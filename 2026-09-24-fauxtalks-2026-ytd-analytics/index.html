FauxTalks in 2026 so far — traffic, SEO, and what to do next

2026-09-24
analyticsseoga4search-consolereddittraffic

A year-to-date review of FauxTalks from 1 Jan to 23 Sep 2026, pulled straight from Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console. September is a partial month; "proj" means projected to a full month.

The short answer

  Traffic grew 3.3x to a June peak, then settled ~25% lower.
  Reddit made the peak. Reddit is now fading.
  Google search that does NOT use the brand name is finally growing — fast.
  The cheapest win left is a title/description rewrite, not new content.

  1. Fewer people search for the name. Brand searches follow how many people

recently heard about the site, and most of them heard via Reddit. 96% of this year's search clicks are branded.

  1. More searches without the name. Impressions went from 33 to 5,392 a

month, and September clicks (189 so far) are already 3.5x August's. This is the first real SEO growth. It is also why overall click-through fell from 77% to 13%: the mix changed, the performance didn't get worse.

The biggest single win

The site already ranks on page 1 for its main terms, but few people click:

A normal click rate at position 6 is 5–8%. The fix is a better homepage title and description. It needs no new content and is conservatively worth +150 clicks a month. It was first recommended on 8 Sep and still hasn't shipped. The three blog posts have the same problem: 640–810 impressions each, but only 3–5 clicks.

Pages and people

  • Home page /: 34.5k landings at 84% engagement.
  • App /app.html direct landings: 18k at 38%. People who land straight in the

app (bookmarks, Reddit links) leave more than twice as often. The empty first screen needs a faster "aha".

  • Platform landing pages (Discord, iMessage, and others) bring very little traffic and have dropped out of Google.
  • Top countries: US 13.6k · India 5.0k · Philippines 3.5k · UK 2.8k · Germany 2.2k.
  • Likely bots: Ukraine (36% engagement), China (31%), and 4.8k sessions with no

landing page (6%).

  • Devices: mobile 69% engagement, desktop 55%, with about equal sessions.
  • A copy of the site with the GA tag (/5776-chat/fauxtalks/index.html, 136 views from 1 user).

Harmless, but it slightly inflates the numbers.

What to do, in order

#PriorityAction
1HighRewrite the homepage and blog titles/descriptions around "fake screenshot / fake chat screenshot generator"
2HighPost on Reddit every 4–6 weeks. It drove the peak, and brand search follows it
3MediumImprove the empty first screen for people who land directly in the app
4MediumSet up Bing Webmaster Tools. Bing is the only search engine still growing
5LowFilter bot countries and "(not set)" out of reports
